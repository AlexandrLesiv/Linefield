## Locale, motion and input requirements

Follow [i18n](../docs/internationalization.md) and [locale scenarios](../docs/testing/internationalization.md).
Include real en/uk, expanded LTR/RTL, mixed-script/CJK, numeric-locale and enlarged-text cases.
Preserve artwork geometry, native field/IME priority and locale-independent IDs. A named proficient
Ukrainian reviewer supplies linguistic approval; resource parity and screenshots do not.

Follow [motion](../docs/design/motion.md), [S00D](S00D.md) and [input inventory](../docs/input/inventory.md).
Specify purpose, interruption, commit/cancel, reduced motion, focus timing and host limitations
together with appearance. Browser tests remain distinct from physical input/native E2E. No
Computer Use or foreground reservation is granted by this task.

Follow [color representations](../docs/design/color-representations.md) when a color-control slice
is authorized. Keep representations separate from document color space and future gamut behavior.
The current unavailable color entry does not authorize a picker or change existing macOS behavior.
