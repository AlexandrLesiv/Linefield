## Outcome and bounded scope

Use the proven repaint operation to avoid replaying completed strokes and the entire active
stroke on every update. Retain one main-thread Rust editor and one CanvasKit worker. Introduce
the reviewed two finite capture records, stable chunk identities, bounded transfer buffers,
priority/fairness scheduler and committed/active image handoff. Keep existing screen/input
behavior, localization and history. No guard-region coverage feature, custom raster tiles,
alternate renderer, second worker, persistence or native implementation in this task.
Use resumable bounded contributor scans initially; the spatial index and retained offscreen
tiles are R03. All renderer paths use the proven bounded operation so remaining scene work
cannot block fresh ink with an unchanged whole-path draw.

Fresh ink and mandatory prior-stroke work must fit the existing input-to-complete-preview
deadline, including dense background work. End A admits B immediately; B cannot appear over
a base missing A. Cancel B preserves A. Semantic obligations cannot be coalesced away. Bound
completed-tail bookkeeping by a canonical target endpoint rather than a bitmap queue; measure
its oldest pending work. Preserve one-stroke Undo and Rust authority on every failure.
