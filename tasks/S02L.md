# Durable library

**Status:** implementation blocked · **Task:** S02L (milestone)
[Structured record](S02L/task.json) · no implementation PR yet.

## What this delivers

Keep drawings in an app-managed library without a filename prompt. Create, list, open, rename
and reopen must preserve drawing identity and durable state. Web leads implementation; macOS
is the next native target. The current gallery holds disposable UI state, not saved drawings.

## What needs a decision

The [concrete empty-library scope](../docs/reviews/durable-library-scope.md) remains for owner
approval. Storage adoption is blocked: the synthetic experiment exposed data loss with damaged
metadata, and safe proxy retirement is unproved. [S02-P01](S02-P01.md) investigates preservation;
healthy-case passes do not resolve these failures.

## First implementation boundary

The proposed first slice is create/list/open/rename/reopen of empty drawings. Delete and real
stroke editing are separate slices. This milestone's later removal requirement remains same-slot
Undo until successful departure; busy drawings and pending exports need their reviewed safeguards.
No folders, sync, multi-selection or external-file write-through is added.

## Acceptance

- LIBRARY-CREATE-001: unnamed creation survives restart and reopen.
- LIBRARY-RENAME-001: durable Unicode rename preserves identity and native text behavior.
- LIBRARY-OPEN-001: failed or cancelled opening preserves the accepted route and content.
- LIBRARY-DELETE-001: later removal/Undo obeys the reviewed crash and ownership rules.
- LIBRARY-FAILURE-001: storage/recovery failures remain visible; preserve ambiguous data.
- LIBRARY-NAV-001: stable layout, scroll and focus; keyboard, accessibility and reduced motion.

These are planned requirements, not passing tests. The [contract](../docs/interfaces/durable-library.md),
[recovery rules](../docs/architecture/recovery.md), [removal contract](../docs/database/removal.md)
and [owner decisions](../docs/decisions.md) retain the full authority and failure requirements.
