# S00C Focused competitor research

Status: planned; role and workflow documented, research report not yet completed.

Outcome: give the owner and ui_ux_designer a sourced comparison for the first macOS preview,
covering the owner-selected trio: Concepts.app, ProDrafts and Apple Freeform for macOS.
See the [catalog](../docs/research/competitors.md); other comparators are for later focused studies. No parity roadmap.

Owners: competitor_researcher produces the report; lead integrates; ui_ux_designer owns workflow synthesis and visual/native adaptations;
macos_ui assesses native feasibility. Owner decides new scope and approves identified designs.

Dependencies: S00 target-user/design priorities. Run before detailed screen design; shared
foundation inventory may proceed in parallel. Research does not block unrelated contract drafts.

Initial questions: pen/tool discoverability; single-stroke selection/move/uniform scaling;
viewport navigation; undo/cancellation and native text focus; save/recovery feedback; accessibility
and localization evidence. Include pitfalls and rejected alternatives, not only attractive UI.
Focus evidence on approved scope; layers, eraser, text, sync or radial palettes remain proposals.

Follow [research workflow](../docs/research/README.md). Required packet: dated source/platform/
version table, three to five ranked recommendations with alternatives/costs/confidence,
designer disposition, feasibility notes and owner decision requests. Map CT/INV/SU/scenarios
where applicable. Public manuals alone do not establish hands-on behavior or performance.

Acceptance: claims traceable to sources; unknowns labeled; owner and designer receive the same
packet; scope classification explicit; new UI still waits for Figma approval. Stop after report
and handoff. No app installation, code, Figma implementation or ongoing monitoring implied.

Decision 148 identifies Concepts as the primary directional reference for an open-source,
cross-platform editable vector sketcher, MacBook first. Compare natural sketching/editability and
native desktop input; do not infer feature parity, proprietary internals or future touch hardware.
Use official workflow sources; no copying assets or unapproved license/distribution decisions.
