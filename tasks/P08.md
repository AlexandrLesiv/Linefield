## Selected offline viewer direction — decision 195

WV1-A is owner-selected: explicit Keep on Watch downloads of iPhone-rendered overview/detail
images, prepared/transferred ahead of use. Native watch UI displays the completed downloaded
revision without requiring the phone nearby. Optional connected updates cannot block offline open.
No bundled watch Skia or native vector renderer is required for this initial scope.
Keep the previous complete revision until replacement is validated and installed safely.
Pinned offline downloads are persistent managed data, not disposable image cache.
Before implementation present exact package/detail guarantees, disk/staging limits, revision
metadata, cancellation/retry/removal/revocation and parser/security limits for owner review.
UX/UI must obtain identified Figma approval for availability/progress/old-version/error states.
Use decision 194's provisional 32 MiB owned-memory/8 MiB disposable-cache profile; persistent
disk quota remains unselected and GPU/framework overhead still needs measurement.
Require paired-device offline open, interrupted updates, low-storage preservation, no mixed
revisions, malformed media, battery/memory and locale/AX evidence. Future network source and
Wear OS architecture are separate scope; no live stream or guaranteed background deadline.
