## Outcome and scope

Determine whether incremental CanvasKit PathOps Union/Intersect over complete-contour groups
of at most 1408 Rust-generated verbs can retain a compact equivalent filled outline for local
repaint, preserving the existing whole-path image and meeting timing gates. Use 128→64→32 backing-pixel regions
and a measured support halo. This is an isolated browser experiment, not production migration
or a retained raster cache. Spatial subdivision alone is already known to fail a stroke
concentrated in one minimum rectangle; the new hypothesis is overlap reduction by Boolean
union. Measure the Boolean operation itself, including cold/evicted reconstruction.

Use existing StrokeState/ScenePage exports of finite scripted prefixes, pausing mutation while
copying and checking scene/token identity. Preserve exported viewport-projected f32 arrays;
partition only at complete-contour boundaries. No new Rust ABI, spatial index, live capture
contract or tile-origin reprojection in this checkpoint. Freeze the unchanged parity oracle
before code. Owner approval covers this experiment and its 4 ms target/8 ms maximum synchronous
unit guard, not a final ABI or AA-policy change. Full scans may supply contributors; report
scan/copy/preparation separately and do not claim they meet production query/input limits.
PathOps output is disposable renderer geometry, never canonical stroke data. A null, slow or
visually different union fails. No Canvas2D brush, second CanvasKit instance, smoothing,
sample loss or tolerance relaxation.
One worker is sufficient; use existing single-owner semantics and no new capture lifetime.

## Current finding and disposition

First case: 256 coincident samples at 1024×768, default full-width 8-unit black pen.
Rust emitted 1536 verbs, partitioned into two complete-contour groups (maximum 1404 verbs).
Reference repeatability was exact across 3,145,728 RGBA channels. The first Boolean
Union(empty accumulator, first group) did not report completion before the five-second
inactivity watchdog terminated its worker. No intersection, candidate drawing or export
result was obtained. This is a bounded failure of the tested grouping strategy, not an exact
five-second PathOps measurement or a general rejection of CanvasKit/other union strategies.
Candidate AA/parity, later halos, retracing, large workloads, rotation, p95 and live-input
acceptance remain unrun. Do not start R02 or migrate caches from this result.
