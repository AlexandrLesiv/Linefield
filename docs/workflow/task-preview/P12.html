P12 Wear OS

Status: target and documentation scaffold authorized 2026-10-16; no implementation.

Host: apps/wearos. Engineering owner: android_ui. UX/UI design follow docs/design/roles.md.
Owner selected view-only access (decision 152); obtain identified feature/design review and implementation approval.

Dependencies: macOS S08 acceptance, reviewed Android/Rust typed adapter, explicit implementation
direction and declared supported Wear OS devices/SDK/toolchain. Early design/feasibility allowed.

Before implementation decide:
- Watch-native toolkit. Google recommends [Compose for Wear OS](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/compose).
Review its benefits and the Wear-specific component and lifecycle differences from phone/tablet Compose Material 3; no framework is adopted now.
- Standalone/companion dependency and document acquisition, freshness/offline behavior, bounded
local cache. Follow [standalone guidance](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/apps/standalone-apps);
phone presence, network/auth or transfer transport is not assumed from watchOS's plan.
- Shared viewer capability/schema conformance and surface/Skia feasibility, size/memory/battery
limits. No new renderer or custom command transport may be introduced silently.
- Round/small layout, touch/rotary input, native Back/swipe dismissal, text scaling/TalkBack,
reduced motion, app suspension/resume and stopped drawing work when not visible.
- Retained-view safety after revoked/missing sources; privacy-safe logs and real-device evidence.

Evidence: actual build/device or explicitly labeled emulator checks; shared VIEWER-001 for approved viewer
scope, ROUTE/MOTION and applicable I18N scenarios, read-only rejection in Rust,
loading/error/offline/recreation, native focus and resource measurements. Missing hardware is
not a pass. Tiles, complications, watch faces and network features are separate scopes.

English and Ukrainian are required launch languages; English is source/fallback. OS-selected language with no in-app switch, locale-format and pseudo-RTL
readiness still apply. Use [i18n](../docs/internationalization.md), [navigation](../docs/navigation.md),
[motion](../docs/design/motion.md), [shared E2E](../docs/testing/shared-e2e.md) and [quality](../docs/quality.md).
Ask before foreground checks; no installs/security changes or runtime implementation now.

Launch acceptance includes I18N-LAUNCH-002/I18N-UK-002 on this host, including viewer UI where applicable. See docs/testing/internationalization.md; no resources or execution evidence exist yet.
