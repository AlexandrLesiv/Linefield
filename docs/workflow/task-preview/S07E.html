Selected export bounds

Decision 36 selects all artwork plus a small documented margin. Include full pressure-derived
stroke widths, transforms, caps/joins and reviewed antialiasing extent. EXPORT-001 must cover
viewport-independent output, distant marks, transformed strokes and selection/prediction exclusion.
Review margin/rounding, revision capture/unfinished interactions, empty content and resource caps
before implementation. Preflight pixel/byte/work limits; fail clearly without silent cropping,
omission or document mutation. No viewport selector or custom crop tool is approved. Exact PNG
multiplier/rounding and identified design remain pending; decisions 37/38 select light background
and fixed pixels-per-logical-unit export scale.

Decision 37 extends EXPORT-001 with the shared fixed light canvas background, opaque composited
output including margins, correct edge/color handling and identical artwork under system-theme
changes. Review exact color/color space and comparison tolerances with canvas contracts. Do not
add transparency controls or assume pure white without design approval.

Decision 38 extends EXPORT-001 with fixed pixels-per-logical-unit dimensions independent of
viewport/display density. Review exact multiplier, margin rounding/minimum dimensions and empty
content. Check arithmetic/allocation limits before rendering; no silent downscaling/cropping or
omission. Verify dimensions with an independent decoder, fractional/extreme bounds, transformed
stroke widths and zoom/density parity. Do not introduce resolution/preset/DPI controls.

Decision 47 extends EXPORT-001: PNG must use each stroke's stored color/width/approved pen
properties and transform, not current toolbar settings. Verify mixed-property drawings and
scaled pressure strokes against the reviewed canvas/color contract; no shared-style substitution.

Decision 48 resolves output color space to sRGB. Review exact channel/alpha encoding and PNG
color metadata; EXPORT-001 checks metadata with an independent decoder and compares canvas/
export colors using agreed tolerances, including antialiased edges on the approved background.
Future wider-gamut export requires a separate reviewed extension preserving old sRGB artwork.

Decision 51 extends EXPORT-001: PNG contains latest completed artwork at snapshot capture,
including pending/failed-autosave edits, with later edits excluded from that captured export.
Compare an independent decode against the same snapshot, not a newer live scene. Export success
cannot clear library pending/failure status or advance its saved revision. Test cancellation and
destination failure separately. Active-gesture availability remains pending; temporary geometry,
predictions and selection overlays never become exported artwork.

Decision 52 resolves active-gesture availability: disable PNG export during stroke/move/scale
and enforce shared Rust rejection. EXPORT-001 verifies no panel, queued export, auto-commit/cancel
or history mutation, then explicit export after end/cancel. Test panel/gesture races under S02
and immutable already-captured exports while later edits occur. Native action tests remain a
separate evidence lane from injected boundary tests.

Decision 53 extends EXPORT-001: enable blank PNG export for an empty completed drawing at the
owner-selected 1920 x 1080 pixels (decision 54), filled with the opaque fixed light sRGB background.
Test new empty drawing, last-stroke deletion, Undo/Redo transitions and a valid point-like stroke.
Verify dimensions/color/opacity independently and invariance under window/zoom/pan/display changes.
Do not create artwork, change history/save status or bypass active-gesture/resource guards. The
empty fallback is separate from nonempty bounds/margins/scale; no size controls or page model.

Decision 54 requires an independent decoder to report exactly 1920 pixels wide by 1080 high
for empty output. No extra margin or scale multiplication; verify opaque fixed light sRGB
background and identical dimensions across viewport/window/display configurations.

Decision 63 extends EXPORT-001 with native replacement confirmation, alternate name/cancel,
prepared PNG validation before replace, target races and injected generation/publication failures.
Verify old destination bytes remain intact before successful replacement and independently decode
the new artifact. Keep library state unchanged and test native focus/confirmation separately from
storage fault injection. Follow S02 provider/interruption guarantees rather than assuming all
filesystems or destinations support identical atomic replacement.

Decision 80 fixes nonempty PNG output at one pixel per logical canvas unit, applied to reviewed
all-artwork bounds plus margin. Test fractional/extreme bounds and transformed stroke extents;
independently decode output dimensions. Window/zoom/display density must not affect dimensions.
Preserve the exact empty 1920 by 1080 fallback and pre-allocation resource checks. Margin/rounding
and resource thresholds still need S02 review; do not silently downscale or crop oversized output.

Decision 81 requires fixed 16-unit padding outside full transformed/pressure-derived visible
stroke bounds on each side. EXPORT-001 includes integer and fractional bounds, negative positions,
point strokes and all four borders. A 1000 by 600 bounds rectangle has 1032 by 632 continuous padded
extent before reviewed rounding. No added padding for the exact empty PNG fallback; no proportional
margin or margin UI. Independently decode dimensions and visually verify uncropped framing.

Decision 102 approves a small bounded FIFO of export requests. Plan per-job state, capacity and
aggregate snapshot/working-memory limits, cancellation and destination-ordering tests. Reject excess
requests explicitly; do not create parallel workers or silently retarget revision/destination.
S02 must review exact capture/admission and native-panel/overwrite timing. Test multiple queued
requests, active/queued cancellation, failed jobs, same destination and stale completions. Decision
103 selects normal finish-on-quit with explicit safe cancellation for all accepted jobs. design review
still requires approval.

Decisions 103/109: test normal Quit waiting for active/queued exports, explicit cancellation,
failure/stall status, completed-publication races and independent save guards. Preserve existing
destination on prepublication cancel; do not remove completed exports. For nonempty output use
padded artwork origin and upward-rounded dimensions, retaining fractional geometry and nominal
16-unit margin. Verify fractional/negative/transformed/pure-translation fixtures and overflow;
empty output remains 1920 by 1080. Floating-point document color does not settle PNG bit depth.

Decision 120 requires independently verified 8-bit PNG channel output and unchanged floating-point
native sRGB values after export. Test intermediate/edge colors, color metadata, opacity and selected
dimensions; exact conversion/rounding remains S02 review. No bit-depth selector or implicit native
quantization. Multi-process storage policy (116) must not allow destination/document ownership
races; exact export destination coordination remains reviewed separately from queue policy.

Decisions 121-126: capture immutable completed artwork at accepted Export before the destination
panel, retaining it across later edits/navigation; cancellation releases the reviewed reservation.
Test command/panel/capacity races and status outside the original editor after design review approval.
Allow same-destination jobs, serialize publication, renew confirmation where needed and verify
changed targets/aliases/concurrent processes. A single failure preserves its outcome and permits
unrelated safe queued work. No collapse to latest job or blind overwrite retry.

Durable job intent/source must recover across crashes using the reviewed S02 schema/snapshot/
privacy/durability design. Test every prepare/publish/ack boundary, unknown completion, missing
permissions, corrupt job data, quotas and stale workers; never retarget to newer drawing contents.
Count cap is three accepted active/waiting/reserved jobs plus byte/work bounds; process/library
scope is pending. Source deletion, recovery prompt and terminal retention policies remain pending.

Batch 8 approved durable-export policies (decisions 132-138)

Use a separate SQLite job database and dedicated immutable snapshot area; exact schema source/source
representation still needs review. Accepted Export captures before its panel in bounded memory;
persist after destination confirmation, with an honest preparing/durable boundary. Automatically
resume only validated safe recovered work; pause uncertain publication/access and changed targets.
Changed destinations need renewed consent or another destination. Release terminal job-only
snapshots after safe durable reconciliation, retaining bounded outcomes and live references;
exact-source retry is unavailable after release. Source deletion explicitly confirms canceling
outstanding exports and coordinates safe cancellation before deletion; published files survive.

The count cap is three accepted active/waiting/reserved jobs PER PROCESS (Q54 B), alongside shared
resource protections. No global count/byte budget or new worker topology is chosen. Specify abandoned
job claims/reassignment, recovered admission, cross-database deletion/job lifecycle and idempotent
cleanup before code. Do not drop durable jobs, blindly retry uncertain overwrites or bypass consent.
Q44 maintenance ownership and batch 9 naming remain pending. Policies are not implementation.
