# Gallery competitor screen references

Research date: 2026-10-17. Scope: public gallery, home, menu and viewer imagery for Linefield visual exploration.
The competitor researcher visually inspected all eight referenced images in the browser. No competitor apps were installed or tested.
Screenshots are linked to their publishers, not downloaded into the repository. They remain third-party references, not Linefield artwork.
Static imagery cannot establish hover behavior, animation timing, accessibility, performance or the original designers' intentions.

The old visual reference board was retired with the gallery experiments. Publisher sources below remain available. [Improvement plan](gallery-improvement-plan.md)

## Concepts

Platform: iPad gallery. Figure: Concepts Gallery interface overview.

[Source page](https://concepts.app/en/manual/gallery) · [Actual source image](https://cdn.tophatch.com/cms/uploads/image348_1776182259432.png)

- Observed in public imagery: Repeated artwork previews and captions; New Drawing is a bottom floating pill.
- Inference: Consistent alignment may help users scan a visual library.
- Proposed adaptation: Adapt tile alignment and artwork prominence. Use our first-square New tile, not its bottom pill.
- Limitations: Screenshot build unknown. Folders, sorting and account features are not Linefield scope.

## Adobe Fresco

Platform: Tablet home screen. Figure: Home screen with quick-start tiles.

[Source page](https://helpx.adobe.com/fresco/desktop/introduction/getting-started-with-user-interface.html) · [Actual source image](https://helpx-prod.scene7.com/is/image/HelpxProdLoc/home-screen-panel?$pjpeg$&jpegSize=300&wid=1600)

- Observed in public imagery: Creation entry points and separate navigation/content areas.
- Inference: Separating creation from browsing can clarify the next action.
- Proposed adaptation: Use clear creation hierarchy and grouped card anatomy; omit templates, tutorials and marketing.
- Limitations: Article dated September 30, 2026; screenshot build unknown. Do not infer platform support from URL path.

## Procreate

Platform: iPad gallery. Figure: Share, Duplicate, Delete gallery action strip.

[Source page](https://help.procreate.com/procreate/handbook/gallery/gallery-organize) · [Actual source image](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/skm640rd/production/0e4c79f9e278acdd5a68fa14064411966c856424-1000x750.jpg?auto=format&auto=format&w=640)

- Observed in public imagery: Dark surrounding chrome, artwork previews and a contextual action strip.
- Inference: Quiet chrome may keep attention on artwork while actions remain nearby.
- Proposed adaptation: Explore artwork-led composition and native contextual actions. Keep only approved actions.
- Limitations: Screenshot build unknown. Share, Duplicate, stacks and gesture bindings are not authorized by this reference.

## Apple Freeform

Platform: macOS gallery. Figure: Favorites marked in All Boards.

[Source page](https://support.apple.com/guide/freeform/create-a-board-frfm9474646a9/5.0/mac/27) · [Actual source image](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/6c2e4ae3-811c-4d5a-a6cc-ccd9c7dd9fa0)

- Observed in public imagery: Native desktop window composition with board previews and navigation regions.
- Inference: Familiar platform structure may reduce the effort to understand browsing.
- Proposed adaptation: Use native titlebar/toolbar hierarchy and typography; omit unapproved sidebar categories.
- Limitations: Guide URL identifies version 5.0/macOS 27; installed build not inspected. Favorites/search are out of scope.

## Adobe Photoshop

Platform: Windows desktop home. Figure: Photoshop Home screen.

[Source page](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/get-started/learn-the-basics/homescreen-overview.html) · [Actual source image](https://helpx-prod.scene7.com/is/image/HelpxProd/The-Photoshop-Home-screen-shows-tabs-like-Home--Le?$pjpeg$&jpegSize=300&wid=1600)

- Observed in public imagery: Dark desktop home surface with navigation and file-browsing regions.
- Inference: A clear separation of chrome and content may help a dense library remain readable.
- Proposed adaptation: Study dark-tone hierarchy and metadata alignment; keep Linefield's smaller feature scope.
- Limitations: Article dated February 23, 2026; screenshot build unknown. Cloud/account/learning areas are not a pattern to copy wholesale.

## Goodnotes

Platform: Apple document creation menu. Figure: Folder option in the New menu.

[Source page](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/15303674514191-Organize-folders-and-documents-in-Goodnotes) · [Actual source image](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/article_attachments/15303674513935)

- Observed in public imagery: A focused crop of a document creation menu, not a full gallery screenshot.
- Inference: A clear menu hierarchy can keep secondary creation choices understandable.
- Proposed adaptation: Study menu spacing and grouping only. Linefield New creates a drawing directly.
- Limitations: Exact device/build unknown. Do not introduce folders, templates or an unnecessary New submenu.

## Apple Photos

Platform: watchOS image viewer. Figure: Single photo with Back and Share overlays.

[Source page](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/watch/apd5c3c53180/27/watchos/27) · [Actual source image](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/6f1dc0cd-7f2b-4047-a84e-de521dba8d46)

- Inference: Content dominance is useful on a small display.

## UX and UI handoff

The lead passed the four core gallery references to both UX and UI, who returned a scoped synthesis.
The extended Photoshop, Goodnotes, Photos and ProDrafts references are supplementary material for the next production brief.

| Variant | Reference | Proposed design inference and adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native Gallery | Freeform | Native window hierarchy, balanced square grid, no invented navigation destinations |
| Native Showcase | Procreate | Large artwork previews with quiet chrome and separate captions |
| Graphic Index | Concepts | Repeated tile/caption alignment and stronger compact hierarchy |
| Graphic Cards | Fresco | Distinct navigation/content/metadata regions, without templates or promotions |
| Warm Mat | Concepts | Adapt sketchbook whitespace into warm app-owned surrounds and opaque artwork mats |
| Warm Folio | Procreate | Spacious framing and caption planes, without stacks or new organization features |

These are our hypotheses, not claims about competitor designers' reasoning or measured usability improvements.
Native Liquid Glass, Material and Fluent mappings still come from platform guidance and engineer feasibility review.
The owner's first-square New tile overrides Concepts' observed bottom-pill placement.
The owner's one-drawing vertical watch paging overrides the different navigation in the Photos reference.

UX adapts artwork prominence, clear creation hierarchy and contextual action discovery; defers templates,
folders, sorting, Share/Duplicate and accounts; rejects literal identical layouts across watches/TV/desktops.
UI carries six distinct compositions into all nine hosts, each with matching Light/Night content and state coverage.
Every Web variant is to include the semi-interactive disposable-fixture prototype described in the improvement plan.
