Portrait screenshots are 774×1700 (54 captures) or 774×1702 (2 captures) mirrored-window pixels, not native device points. The initial study used Mirroring with Developer Mode off. The owner later enabled Developer Mode for a separate timing investigation; those results are recorded separately. User authorized this session and completed Mirroring setup. No physical Pencil, pressure, multitouch, VoiceOver or latency evidence. Existing drawings preserved; mutations confined to the newly created Linefield research 18 Oct drawing. Local report only.

Every CS-OBS finding below is a single-session observation, medium confidence until reproduced directly on device. Official-source packet CS-DOC findings remain separate. An observation is not a benchmark, semantic conformance test or universal platform claim.
